The small things

The details that make it a daily tool.

Notes on every card

Open a card to write down the details. The title stays short, the notes hold the rest.

Drag on any screen

Mouse, trackpad or thumb. On a phone, press and hold a card to pick it up.

Keyboard friendly

Enter adds a card, Esc closes what's open. Your hands can stay where they are.

Light and dark

Follows your system, or flip it from the top bar. Your choice sticks.
