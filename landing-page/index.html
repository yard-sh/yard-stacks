Drag that tracks your hand

Cards lift, tilt the way you throw them, and drop into a gap that opens as you go. Prefer the keyboard? Focus a card and press Cmd + arrow.

As many boards as you keep

One for work, one for the house, one for the thing you will get to eventually. Switch between them from the top bar.

Saves as you type

There is no save button. New cards, renamed columns, and every move persist the moment you make them.

Yours alone

Boards are scoped to your Yard account. Sign-in is handled by Yard, so Stacks never sees a password.
